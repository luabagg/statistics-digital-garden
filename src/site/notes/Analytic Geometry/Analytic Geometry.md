# Analytic Geometry

## Summary

Analytic geometry studies geometric objects with coordinates, equations, and vectors. Distance, midpoints, lines, planes, vectors, and conic sections connect algebra to geometry and prepare the way for multivariable calculus and linear algebra.

## Prerequisites

[[Pre-Calculus/Functions/Functions\|Functions]] and basic coordinate geometry. Helpful: [[Pre-Calculus/Trigonometry/Trigonometry\|Trigonometry]], [[Analytic Geometry/Completing the Square\|Completing the Square]].

## Learning order

1. [[Analytic Geometry/Distance and Midpoint\|Distance and Midpoint]] — metric structure of the plane and space
2. [[Analytic Geometry/Completing the Square\|Completing the Square]] — rewrite quadratics for centers and radii
3. [[Analytic Geometry/Lines and Planes\|Lines and Planes]] — parametric and Cartesian forms
4. [[Analytic Geometry/Vectors and Dot Product\|Vectors and Dot Product]] — direction, length, angles, projections
5. [[Analytic Geometry/Conic Sections\|Conic Sections]] — circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola

## How to use these notes

- Prefer equations with stated coordinate systems.
- Check assumptions (dimension, nonzero normals/directions) before applying formulas.
- Use completing the square when identifying centers of circles and ellipses.

## Next steps
