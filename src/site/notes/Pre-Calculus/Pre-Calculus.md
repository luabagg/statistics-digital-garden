# Pre-Calculus

## Summary

Pre-calculus builds the function language, algebraic techniques, and trigonometry needed for limits, derivatives, and integrals. This hub organizes the path from functions through polynomials, exponentials/logarithms, and trigonometry into calculus readiness.

## Prerequisites

Prerequisites: high-school algebra (equations, inequalities, graphing on the coordinate plane). Optional geometry background helps with trigonometry.

## Learning order

1. [[Pre-Calculus/Functions/Functions\|Functions]] — domain, range, graphs, composition, inverses
2. [[Pre-Calculus/Polynomials and Rational Functions/Polynomials and Rational Functions\|Polynomials and Rational Functions]] — zeros, end behavior, asymptotes
3. [[Pre-Calculus/Exponentials and Logarithms/Exponentials and Logarithms\|Exponentials and Logarithms]] — growth/decay, log laws, change of base
4. [[Pre-Calculus/Trigonometry/Trigonometry\|Trigonometry]] — unit circle, six ratios, identities, common angles
